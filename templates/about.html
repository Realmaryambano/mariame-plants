Our Story and Roots

Mariamé was born from a lifelong love for nature, inspired by my mother and her beautiful passion for plants. Growing up, I watched her care for every plant with patience and love, transforming ordinary spaces into peaceful little gardens filled with life, color, and warmth. Her love for gardening became the inspiration behind Mariamé.

Today, Mariamé brings together beautiful plants, flowers, quality soil, and charming décor to help you bring a little piece of nature into your home. Our goal is to make plant shopping simple, joyful, and accessible for every plant lover across Pakistan.
